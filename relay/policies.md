# Fernwood Coffee Roasters: customer support policies

## shipping
- Orders ship from Portland, Oregon within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 PM Pacific ship the next business day.
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days. Web orders under $40 pay $5.99 shipping; orders of $40 or more ship free.
- Subscription and wholesale orders always ship free.
- If an order shows "in transit" for more than 8 business days, we send a replacement at no charge.
- If tracking shows an order as delivered but the customer says it never arrived, a person on the team
  investigates with the carrier before offering a replacement or refund.
- We ship to the United States only.

## damaged or wrong items
- If a bag arrives torn, damaged, or the wrong coffee is sent, we replace it free or refund that item, whichever the customer prefers. No need to send it back.
- Report damage within 30 days of delivery. A photo helps but is not required.

## returns
- Unopened coffee and unused equipment can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.
- Opened coffee can't be returned, but if you don't like a coffee, we'll credit one bag of a different coffee once per customer.
- Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items; otherwise the customer pays it.

## refunds
- Approval limit: support can approve on its own a refund, credit or free replacement that another policy
  allows, worth up to $50 per order. Anything worth more than $50, or a second refund on the same order, needs a
  manager's approval.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the whole order was damaged, wrong, or lost.

## subscriptions
- Plans: monthly_1_bag ($16/month), monthly_2_bags ($30/month), biweekly_1_bag ($31/month). Each delivery is a coffee of our choice unless the customer picks one.
- Customers can skip a delivery, pause for up to 2 months, change plan, or cancel at any time before the next billing date, with no fee.
- Changes made less than 2 days before a delivery apply from the following delivery.

## wholesale
- Wholesale is for cafés, restaurants and offices. Prices are 30% off retail on 1kg bags.
- Minimum order: 4 bags. Orders ship free, weekly or every two weeks.
- New wholesale accounts are set up by our wholesale team (wholesale@fernwood.example), usually within 2 business days.

## contact
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM Pacific. Replies within 1 business day.
